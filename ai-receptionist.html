AI receptionist

An AI receptionist for Hawaiʻi businesses

It picks up the calls your team can't get to, answers the simple questions, gets the details right and gets each caller to the right person. Your people keep the conversations that need a person.

Try the phone demo Or tell us about your calls

What it does for you

  • It picks up when you can't

    After hours, on weekends and when every line is busy. It doesn't get tired, it doesn't forget what to ask, and it's never too busy with another caller.

  • It gets the details right

    It asks the same questions every time and sends you a clear summary, so nobody has to call back to fill in the blanks.

  • It handles the simple questions

    Hours, directions, what you offer. It answers those itself and sends the calls that need a person to the right one.

  • It books the meeting

    It finds a real open time on your calendar and books it while the caller is still on the phone.

Built for how Hawaiʻi does business

In Hawaiʻi, customer service is everything. People choose a business by how it treats them, so we don't build a receptionist to replace your front desk.

We build it to take the low-pressure calls off your team, like basic questions, taking down the details and handing callers off reliably. That gives your people more time and care for the moments that matter most, like a worried customer, a follow-up or anything complicated.

This is home for us, and we've built these long enough to know that's the setup that works here.

Where it works best

It's at its best doing a few jobs the same way every time.

  • After hours

    Evenings, weekends and holidays, when the office is closed and customers still call.

  • Emergency lines for the trades

    Restoration, plumbing, roofing and HVAC. It sorts the true emergencies from the calls that can wait and gets the on-call tech the details right away.

  • Booking for professional offices

    Law, real estate, accounting and consulting, where every new lead means finding a time to meet.

  • The first line of a busy front desk

    It answers the quick questions and passes the rest to your staff, so the people at the desk can focus on the customer in front of them.

A phone call feels like talking to a person, so callers expect quick answers. That's why a good AI receptionist takes on a smaller job than a website chat does, and does it fast.

What it connects to

It works with the tools you already use. It can put meetings on Google Calendar, send text messages, post to Slack and add new leads to Salesforce. If your business runs on something else, ask us.

In use here in Honolulu

Our receptionist has answered a creative technology company's main line in Honolulu since March 2026. It learns what each caller needs, answers questions about the business and books discovery meetings into real open times.

We've also worked with businesses that were using answering services. Staff kept changing, callers heard some version of “Sorry, I'm new,” and calls often came back missing the details the business needed.

Questions owners ask

What happens when a call needs a person?

It takes down the details and gets the caller to the right person, and your team gets everything they need to pick it up from there. Nobody starts from scratch.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. It finds a real open time on your calendar and books it while the caller is still on the phone.

Who keeps it running?

We do. We watch how it handles calls and tune it as your business changes, so your team doesn't have to.

Could we build one ourselves?

You can, if you have someone with both the technical and the customer service know-how to build it and keep it sharp. Every business we've worked with tried first, and by the time they called us they'd spent weeks or months on it.
