What you get back

  • One place for the work

    Requests, schedules and invoices live in one place where everyone can see where things stand, instead of in emails, texts and hallway conversations.

  • The math does itself

    Invoices fill themselves in from the hours worked. Weekly pay adds itself up. The shopping list rounds to the case you actually buy.

  • Each person sees what they need

    The owner sees the whole picture, and each person on the team sees their own schedule, pay and tools.

  • New hires learn faster

    Step-by-step guides and an assistant that knows your procedures mean the answers stop living in one person's head.

If that sounds like your week, an app built around your process takes that work off your team.

Built around your business

Software off the shelf makes you work its way. We start from how your team already works, with your forms, your steps and the sheet you already started, and build only what you'll use.

It sends email and text alerts, makes PDF invoices and connects to the tools you already use. After launch, new tools get added without disturbing the ones people already rely on.
