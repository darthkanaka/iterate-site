Some of our work

Three builds for Hawaiʻi organizations, told plainly: the problem, what we built, and what happened.

01
AI phone receptionist

A family roofing contractor on Oʻahu

Confirmations went live
The problem
Calls that came in after hours, or while the crew was up on a roof, went to voicemail. Every booking also meant someone calling back to confirm the name and the address.
What we built
A voice agent that answers, qualifies the job and books the inspection onto the real calendar. Behind it, a text confirmation system that checks each booking with the customer and hands any conversation back to a person with one click.
What happened
The text confirmation system went live and handled real bookings, checking the calendar every five minutes. The voice and scheduling pieces were built and tested alongside the business's existing setup.

02
AI knowledge base

A Hawaiʻi handyman company

In daily use
The problem
Everything a new hire needed lived in people's heads: how to answer the phone, how to write a quote, what comes first each day, and how to do the actual job.
What we built
A private knowledge base of the company's own procedures with a chat interface on top. Staff ask in plain words and get the answer the business would give.
What happened
New hires train from it and returning staff use it as a reference. The procedures inside keep getting updated as the business learns.
  • Supabase
  • AI chat
  • Company SOPs
03
Content and knowledge platform

A Hawaiʻi nonprofit

In daily use
The problem
A small team writing grants, training curriculum, marketing and donor communications, with every first draft landing on the same one or two people.
What we built
Five AI agents on top of the organization's own documents, each with its own lane: grants, training, marketing, operations and a general assistant. Documents sync live from Google Drive, and staff can tune how each agent behaves without a code change.
What happened
The team uses it as the first stop for drafts, so only the harder, strategic work rolls up. Now in development: an inventory and purchasing system for their operations.
