Scheduling and pay for a small team

A team portal in the style of the one we built, running on a made-up creative studio called Brightreef. The schedule feeds each contractor's invoice, the owner approves it, and it lands in the finance numbers. Switch between the owner and a contractor to see both sides. Nothing you change is saved.

  1. As Jesse, tell Mele you can't make one of your shifts.
  2. Switch to Mele. Fix the double-booking and cover the open shifts on the schedule board.
  3. Back as Jesse, send this period's invoice. It fills itself in from the shifts he worked.
  4. As Mele, approve it, then check Finance for this month, this quarter and this year.
Built for this business

From the schedule to the books

  • The schedule does the paperwork

    Shifts turn into invoice hours on their own, so nobody retypes a timesheet at the end of the week.

  • It works with the calendar you already use

    Shifts sync to Google Calendar, so everyone keeps the phone reminders and alerts they're used to. Nobody learns a new calendar.

  • Problems find you first

    Double-bookings, call-outs and long weeks get flagged before they turn into a missed shoot.

  • The numbers are always current

    Approve an invoice and this month's spend updates. Month, quarter or year to date, no spreadsheet.
