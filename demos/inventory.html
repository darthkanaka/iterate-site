Inventory for a small kitchen

This is how the inventory system we're building for a nonprofit's kitchen works, running on a made-up bakery and plate lunch spot called Two Scoop Kitchen. Change what they're making this week and watch the shopping list rewrite itself. Nothing you change is saved.

Built for this business

Only what this kitchen needs

  • It counts the way a kitchen counts

    Two and a half bags of flour, not 125 pounds. The system does the conversion.

  • The plan drives everything

    Change the week's menu and the shopping list, the order sizes and the cost per plate all follow.

  • Nothing in here goes unused

    Stock, the week's plan and the shopping list. No reports nobody reads and no settings nobody touches.
