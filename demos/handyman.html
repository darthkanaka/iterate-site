One app for the office and the crew

A working copy of the app we built for a handyman company, running on a made-up one called Coralgate Home Services. Switch between the owner and a tech to see both sides. Nothing you change is saved.

  1. As Leilani, ask Kai what's on today, or who owes the company money.
  2. Open Tech pay. Assign the invoice with no tech on it, finalize Jordan's lines and mark someone paid.
  3. Switch to Malia. Walk through a field guide, then check My pay and leave the office a note.
Built for this business

The office and the field, on the same page

  • The office asks, the assistant answers

    Today's schedule, open requests, who owes money and a draft quote, pulled from the systems the business already runs on.

  • Every job done the same way

    Step-by-step guides with the gear, the safety checks and the flat-rate price built in, so a new hire works like a veteran.

  • Pay works itself out

    Each tech's share comes straight from the invoices. The office checks it, locks it and marks it paid.

  • Everyone sees only their part

    A tech gets the guides and their own pay. The office gets the rest. Nobody wades through screens that aren't for them.
